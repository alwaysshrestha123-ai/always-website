About Me

Hello

I'm Alwaysss : storyteller, light-chaser, frequent flyer.

What started as a camera and a one-way ticket became a career telling stories across 18 countries and 3 continents. Today I create photography and film for hotels, hospitality brands, restaurants, travel companies and personal brands who want imagery that feels like cinema, not stock.

My philosophy is simple: the best images aren't taken, they're felt. I wait for the golden hour, the quiet moment, the real laugh. Whether it's a clifftop villa in Italy or a street kitchen in Hanoi, I look for the feeling of a place , then build the frame around it.

Who I work with

Luxury & boutique hotels · Resorts & retreats · Restaurants & cafés · Travel & tourism brands · Commercial & lifestyle brands · Founders & personal brands · DMOs & tourism boards

Where I work

Based in Canberra at the moment, working worldwide, across Asia, Europe and beyond. Happy to travel to you, or meet you somewhere beautiful in between.
